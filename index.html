New in v0.0.5

Released 20 November 2026. This page skipped v0.0.3 and v0.0.4, so their changes are listed here too.

  • Relationships tree. Select a part and the inspector lists only the parts it is linked to by constraints or joints, from the base down. The bar of every constraint along the bottom of the screen is gone.
  • Product page links. Thorlabs parts in your library get a Product page link in the inspector, even when they were imported from a folder of STEP files.
  • Drag ghost for imported parts. Parts imported from STEP now show a translucent preview while you drag them from the library onto a table.
  • Click again to deselect. Clicking a selected part, in the scene or the scene tree, clears the selection. The typed-value box now appears only once a part actually moves.
  • Fixes. A part file with a reversed slide range no longer crashes placement, and Save no longer hangs when the 3D view stops answering.

Since v0.0.2

  • Bundles: save a set of parts to the library and place it anywhere.
  • Selection lock: locked parts are skipped by clicks; Shift+click still picks them.
  • Laser on/off toggle in the inspector, and a starter laser part.
  • Constraints re-solve when you nudge or rotate a part from the keyboard.
  • labsetup-cli: a headless command-line tool for project files, in the same zip.
  • The beam-height stack builder panel was removed; build post stacks by dragging parts onto each other.

What it does

Everything below is in the download. The close-ups come from earlier builds; the parts of the window they show work the same way in v0.0.5.

3D, top view, or both

The same table as a 3D scene or a dimensioned top view with the hole grid, labels and the beam path. Split shows them side by side, and both update together as you move parts.

Constraints and mating

Mate, flush, concentric and angle constraints between parts. Pick a face or an axis on each part; posts seat in holders, holders on bases, mounts on posts, and the chain stays together when you move it.

Your vendor CAD

Import a STEP file, a Thorlabs item URL or a whole folder of STEP files into the library. Imported parts snap to table holes and mate like built-in ones.

What labs use it for

Plan an experiment before buying parts
Lay out the optics on a table of the right size, check that the beam reaches every element, and see how much room is left. Nothing is bolted down yet.
Check beam heights and clearances
The dimensioned top view shows where every part sits on the hole grid of a crowded table; the 3D view shows what stands in the beam.
Lay out a whole lab room
Draw the room, add doors and windows, and place tables, racks and breadboards where they will really go.
Hand over a parts list and a figure
Export the bill of materials as CSV, grouped by vendor and part number (example), and the current view as a PNG up to 4K for a proposal or a paper.
